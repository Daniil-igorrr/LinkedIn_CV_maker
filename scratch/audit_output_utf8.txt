     1	# Anti-Hallucination Rules & Verification Checklist
     2	
     3	> [!CRITICAL]
     4	> Factual integrity is absolute. Under no circumstances should you extrapolate, embellish, or fabricate credentials, metrics, titles, tools, or employment history.
     5	
     6	---
     7	
     8	## 1. Core Principles
     9	1. **Traceability:** Every single statement, metric, tool, or claim in a generated resume or cover letter **must be directly traceable** to a specific line in `master-profile.md`. If a fact cannot be found in `master-profile.md`, it **must not** be written.
    10	2. **Explicit Non-Facts:** Observe all restrictions listed in the `Explicit non-facts` section of `master-profile.md` (no unlisted employment, no unlisted certifications, no hired corporate SDR titles).
    11	3. **No Guesswork / No Assumptions:** If you are unsure whether a fact in `master-profile.md` satisfies a specific job requirement, **ask the user directly** in chat rather than making assumptions or stretching the truth.
    12	
    13	---
    14	
    15	## 2. Strictly Forbidden (Do NOT Invent)
    16	- ÔØî **Metrics & Numbers:** Do not modify numbers or invent new ones (e.g., quota attainment %, revenue generated, team size, pipeline value). Only use metrics explicitly stated in `master-profile.md` (e.g., 500+ prospects, 5ÔÇô10 qualified leads/week, 3 discovery meetings).
    17	- ÔØî **Company Names & Titles:** Do not invent previous employers or convert independent work into formal hired employment.
    18	- ÔØî **Dates & Timelines:** Do not alter dates of education or projects.
    19	- ÔØî **Tools & Technologies:** Do not add CRM platforms, scraping tools, dialers, or software not explicitly listed in `master-profile.md` (even if standard for SDR roles, e.g., Outreach.io, ZoomInfo, Salesloft, Gong).
    20	- ÔØî **Certifications & Diplomas:** Do not list any certifications (e.g., HubSpot Inbound, Salesforce Certified, etc.).
    21	- ÔØî **Language Fluency:** Do not add languages or alter proficiency levels beyond what is stated.
    22	- ÔØî **Domain Expertise Claims (disguised fabrication):** Do not claim understanding, fluency, literacy, or passion for a domain/technology area (e.g., "AI/cloud infrastructure", "open models", "regulated finance") unless a specific fact in `master-profile.md` directly supports it. Phrases like *"I understand how to position X"*, *"I possess the technical literacy to Y"*, *"passionate about translating Z into business value"* are capability claims, not neutral rephrasing ÔÇö even when they contain no invented tool, number, or employer, they are still fabrication if nothing in `master-profile.md` backs them.
    23	  - **Red flag test:** if a sentence in the CV/cover letter closely echoes or paraphrases the job posting's own description of the ideal candidate (rather than describing something the candidate actually did), that is a strong signal this rule is being violated. The job posting describes what the employer wants ÔÇö it is never itself a source of fact about the candidate.
    24	
    25	---
    26	
    27	## 3. Permitted Actions
    28	- Ô£ö´©Å **Rephrasing:** You may adapt phrasing to align with the vocabulary and keywords of the target job posting, provided the core meaning and facts remain 100% faithful to `master-profile.md`.
    29	- Ô£ö´©Å **Reordering / Prioritization:** You may reorder experience bullets, skills, and summary points so that the most relevant points appear first.
    30	- Ô£ö´©Å **Selective Emphasis:** You may highlight specific aspects of the candidate's experience (e.g., technical scraping vs. email copywriting) depending on the job focus.
    31	- Ô£ö´©Å **Honest Bridging:** When a job posting asks for domain expertise the candidate doesn't have, you may build an honest bridge from an adjacent, real fact ÔÇö but it must explicitly acknowledge the gap, not paper over it. Template: *"While my direct experience has been in [actual domain from master-profile.md], I've built hands-on fluency with [actual tool/skill from master-profile.md] through [actual context] ÔÇö and I'm eager to bring that into [target domain]."* This is permitted because every clause traces to a real fact and the gap is stated honestly, not disguised.
    32	
    33	---
    34	
    35	## 4. Handling Missing Requirements (Gap Report)
    36	- When a job posting requires a skill, certification, tool, domain expertise, or experience level that the candidate does not have in `master-profile.md`:
    37	  - **DO NOT** add it to the resume or cover letter.
    38	  - **DO NOT** attempt to "soften" or simulate the skill in the CV.
    39	  - **DO NOT** paraphrase the job posting's own requirement back as a claimed personal trait or "understanding" ÔÇö rewording a gap into a confident-sounding sentence is still fabrication, not a permitted rephrasing (see Section 2, Domain Expertise Claims).
    40	  - **DO** log it in a dedicated **Gap Report** section in the agent's chat response (outside the generated markdown documents).
    41	
    42	---
    43	
    44	## 5. Mandatory Pre-Output Self-Verification Checklist
    45	Before saving or outputting any CV or Cover Letter, run a line-by-line verification:
    46	
    47	- [ ] **Line Check:** Is every single bullet point and statement directly backed by a line in `master-profile.md`? (Yes / No)
    48	  - *Action on 'No':* Remove the sentence or bullet immediately.
    49	- [ ] **Metrics Check:** Are all numbers (500+, 5ÔÇô10 leads/week, 3 meetings) strictly identical to `master-profile.md`? (Yes / No)
    50	- [ ] **Tools Check:** Are all mentioned tools (Apollo.io, Instantly.ai, Sales Nav, HubSpot, Salesforce, Make.com, Python, ChatGPT, Claude, Antigravity CLI) only from the approved list? (Yes / No)
    51	- [ ] **Role Title Check:** Is the role title accurately represented as "Founder & Lead Generation Specialist ÔÇö CLG Project"? (Yes / No)
    52	- [ ] **Missing Skills Check:** Are missing job requirements excluded from the CV and documented in the Gap Report? (Yes / No)
    53	- [ ] **Domain Expertise Check:** Does any sentence claim understanding, fluency, or passion for a domain/technology without a specific `master-profile.md` fact backing it ÔÇö especially any sentence that closely echoes the job posting's own wording back as a personal trait? (Yes / No)
    54	  - *Action on 'Yes':* Rewrite using the Honest Bridging template (Section 3) or remove and move the requirement to the Gap Report.

     1	# Workflow ÔÇö Step-by-Step Task Execution
     2

     4	
     5	---
     6	
     7	## Step 1: Ingestion & Job Analysis
     8	1. Read the complete job description carefully.
     9	2. Extract and structure the following metadata:
    10	   - **Company Name**
    11	   - **Target Role / Title**
    12	   - **Core Requirements & Responsibilities**
    13	   - **Preferred Tech Stack / Tools**
    14	   - **Key Industry Keywords**
    15	
    16	---
    17	
    18	## Step 2: Cross-Referencing with `master-profile.md`
    19	1. Read `master-profile.md`.
    20	2. Compare each extracted job requirement against the facts in `master-profile.md`:
    21	   - Identify direct matches (skills, tools, achievements, languages).
    22	   - Identify transferable/adjacent matches strictly rooted in existing facts.
    23	   - Identify unfulfilled requirements (gaps).
    24	
    25	---
    26	
    27	## Step 3: Resume Generation
    28	1. Load `templates/resume-template.md`.
    29	2. Populate the template strictly following the fixed section order:
    30	   - **Header / Contact Information:** Taken verbatim from `master-profile.md`.
    31	   - **Professional Summary:** 2ÔÇô3 concise sentences tailored to the role using only verified facts.
    32	   - **Experience:** Prioritize and reorder bullet points from the CLG Project entry to emphasize the most relevant achievements for this role.
    33	   - **Skills:** Group and highlight matching tools and skills from the profile.
    34	   - **Education:** Degree and schedule details from `master-profile.md`.
    35	   - **Availability & Work Authorization:** Verified availability and location preferences.
    36	3. Ensure length fits within 1 standard A4 page.
    37	4. Perform the self-verification checklist from `.agent/rules/anti-hallucination.md`.
    38	
    39	---
    40	
    41	## Step 4: Cover Letter Generation
    42	1. Load `templates/cover-letter-template.md`.
    43	2. Draft a targeted cover letter:
    44	   - **Length:** Strictly 150ÔÇô200 words.
    45	   - **Content:** Include 2ÔÇô3 concrete, verified achievements/metrics from `master-profile.md` that address the employer's key pain points.
    46	   - **Tone:** Confident, direct, factual, and free of fluff.
    47	3. Perform the self-verification checklist from `.agent/rules/anti-hallucination.md`.
    48	
    49	---
    50	
    51	## Step 5: Saving Output Files

    53	- `output/{Company}_{Role}_CV.md`

    61	In the chat output to the user:
    62	1. Provide clickable links to the newly generated CV and Cover Letter in `output/`.

    64	3. If any ambiguous points were encountered, highlight them for user clarification.
