# Output Format & Style Guidelines

This document specifies the formatting, structure, language, and stylistic requirements for all generated documents.

---

## 1. Page Limit & Density
- **Resume Length:** Strictly **1 standard A4 page** (maximum).
- **Density:** Compact, scannable layout. Bullet points should be 1–2 lines each, focusing on action verbs + context + quantitative result.

---

## 2. Section Order (Resume)
The order of sections must strictly follow:
1. **Header & Contact Information**
2. **Professional Summary**
3. **Experience**
4. **Skills**
5. **Education**
6. **Availability & Work Authorization**

*Do not alter, reorder, or omit sections without explicit instruction.*

---

## 3. Language & Localization
- **Output Language:** All CVs and Cover Letters must be written in **English**, regardless of whether the conversation or job posting is in Russian, Spanish, or another language (unless the user explicitly requests a specific localized version).
- **Tone:** Professional, direct, action-oriented, and factual.

---

## 4. Tone & Vocabulary Constraints
- ❌ **No Empty Buzzwords:** Avoid unsubstantiated fluff such as *"results-driven"*, *"passionate"*, *"highly motivated"*, *"hard-working"*, *"dynamic professional"* unless immediately paired with a concrete, verified fact or metric from `master-profile.md`.
- ✔️ **Impact-Driven Phrasing:** Use strong action verbs (e.g., *Built, Sourced, Qualified, A/B tested, Configured, Generated, Converted*).
- ✔️ **Quantified Context:** Highlight metrics (e.g., *500+ prospects*, *5–10 qualified leads/week*, *3 discovery meetings*) wherever relevant to demonstrate execution capability.

---

## 5. Cover Letter Structure
- **Target Word Count:** Strictly **150–200 words**.
- **Structure:**
  1. **Greeting & Targeted Hook:** Why this specific role at this specific company.
  2. **Relevant Proof Points (1–2 paragraphs):** 2–3 specific accomplishments/numbers directly matching job requirements.
  3. **Call to Action & Sign-off:** Clear, polite invitation for a conversation.


## 6. Tailoring Quality — Job-Specific, Not Generic

The CV must be tailored to the CURRENT job description, not merely rewritten from the master profile.

Before drafting, identify the 3–5 most important job requirements for this candidate. Then:
- Put the strongest matching evidence in the Professional Summary and top Experience bullets.
- Prefer concrete actions + context + result over generic traits.
- Use the job's terminology only when it describes a fact actually supported by `master-profile.md`.
- Do not write generic filler such as "highly motivated", "passionate", "eager to succeed", "strong communicator", or "results-driven" unless immediately grounded in a verified fact.
- Do not copy wording or claims from a previous CV/cover letter for another company when the current JD does not support them.
- The Summary should answer: "Why is this candidate relevant to THIS role?" in 2–3 compact sentences.
- Experience bullets should be selected and ordered by relevance; do not mechanically include every master-profile bullet.
- Keep the CV visually balanced. If six bullets make the experience section crowded, prefer the 4–5 most relevant bullets rather than shrinking typography below the project limits.

## 7. Contact Links

The Markdown header MUST preserve the template's Markdown links for email and LinkedIn:
`[email](mailto:email)` and `[linkedin_clean](linkedin_url)`.

The final PDF MUST contain a clickable LinkedIn hyperlink to the candidate's profile. This is verified by the official PDF generator. Never replace the URL with plain text during PDF generation.
