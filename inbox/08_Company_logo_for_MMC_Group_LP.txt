About the job
Sales Development Representative (SDR/BDR)Remote – Austin, TX Area$45,000 Base | Targeted $90,000 Year-One EarningsReady to turn your outbound sales skills into a career in software sales?MMC is hiring multiple Sales Development Representatives for an established, privately owned software company with a strong market presence and a clear path for advancement. This is an opportunity to earn meaningful incentives, work with a modern sales technology stack, and potentially advance into a Junior Account Executive role within six months based on performance.This position is fully remote; however, candidates must live in the Austin area and be available for occasional in-person training and team sessions.What You’ll Do
Connect with business decision-makers through high-volume cold calling, email, LinkedIn, and targeted campaigns
Complete 150+ outbound activities per day, with a strong emphasis on phone outreach
Identify approximately 20 new target prospects daily
Build and manage structured outbound sales sequences
Uncover business challenges and communicate relevant solutions
Generate a goal of five qualified meetings per week for Account Executives
Maintain accurate prospect and activity information in HubSpot
Collaborate with Sales, Marketing, and Revenue Operations to build pipeline
What Will Help You Succeed
One to two years of SDR, BDR, inside sales, lead-generation, or outbound prospecting experience preferred
Confidence making high-volume outbound calls
Strong phone presence, listening skills, and professional communication
Ability to engage decision-makers and build interest quickly
Competitive, coachable, persistent, and motivated by measurable goals
Experience with HubSpot and LinkedIn Sales Navigator is highly preferred
Familiarity with Amplemarket, Instantly, Apollo, or similar prospecting tools is a plus
What’s in It for You?
$45,000 annual base salary
Targeted first-year earnings of approximately $90,000
$75 incentive for each qualified appointment generated, paid monthly
Additional closing-bonus potential
Fully remote work
Established technology, tools, and prospecting resources
Opportunity to advance into a Junior Account Executive role within six months based on performance
If you thrive on the phone, enjoy creating opportunities, and want to build a long-term career in software sales, apply today or contact Calvin Ormerod at cormerod@mmcgrp.com.
