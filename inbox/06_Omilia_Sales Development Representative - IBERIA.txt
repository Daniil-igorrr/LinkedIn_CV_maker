About the job
The Sales Development Representative, IBERIA is responsible for generating qualified pipeline for Omilia's IBERIA enterprise sales team through structured outbound prospecting and inbound lead qualification. The role sits at the top of the sales funnel — identifying, engaging, and qualifying senior decision-makers at target enterprise accounts across Europe, the Middle East, and Africa, and converting interest into discovery meetings for Account Executives. Success is measured by qualified meetings booked, pipeline contribution, and outbound activity quality across assigned IBERIA territories.

Accountabilities

Own the top-of-funnel pipeline generation for assigned IBERIA territories — consistently delivering a volume of qualified meetings that meets or exceeds monthly and quarterly targets
Maintain a structured, data-driven outbound prospecting motion — building and executing multi-touch, multi-channel sequences across phone, email, LinkedIn, and social that reach senior CX, IT, and operations decision-makers at target IBERIA enterprise accounts
Qualify inbound leads from marketing campaigns, events, and website activity — rapidly assessing fit, intent, and timing, and advancing qualified prospects to the appropriate Account Executive
Act as the first commercial representation of Omilia in IBERIA prospect interactions — communicating Omilia's value proposition clearly and compellingly across diverse European markets and cultural contexts
Maintain accurate and complete records of all prospecting activity in Salesforce — ensuring that the Sales Director has full visibility of pipeline generation activity, conversion rates, and outreach performance across the IBERIA region

Key Responsibilities

Outbound Prospecting

Research, identify, and build targeted prospect lists for assigned IBERIA territories — drawing on Salesforce, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, ZoomInfo, and other intelligence tools to map target accounts, identify buying centres, and pinpoint senior decision-makers across CX, IT, contact centre operations, and digital transformation functions
Execute structured multi-touch outbound sequences — combining personalised cold email, phone outreach, LinkedIn engagement, and social selling to engage senior prospects at target IBERIA enterprise accounts across priority verticals including financial services, insurance, telecommunications, utilities, healthcare, and public sector
Craft compelling, personalised outreach in English and relevant IBERIA languages as applicable — connecting Omilia's conversational AI and contact centre capabilities to each prospect's known business challenges and demonstrating genuine understanding of each target account and market context
Conduct high-quality discovery conversations with senior prospects — qualifying on budget authority, need, and timing, uncovering the prospect's contact centre environment and automation objectives, and building sufficient interest and context to secure a substantive first meeting for the Account Executive
Adapt outreach approach and communication style to the distinct procurement cultures and business norms of assigned IBERIA markets — recognising that prospecting into UK and German enterprises, for example, requires meaningfully different tone, cadence, and messaging

Inbound Lead Management

Respond to inbound leads from marketing campaigns, content downloads, event registrations, and website enquiries within agreed SLAs — assessing fit and intent rapidly and advancing qualified prospects through to AE handover
Partner with the marketing team on IBERIA campaign follow-up — ensuring that leads generated from webinars, events, and digital campaigns are worked promptly and effectively, and providing feedback on lead quality and regional relevance to improve targeting

Account Executive Collaboration

Work closely with assigned IBERIA Account Executives to align prospecting activity to territory priorities — understanding each AE's target account list, preferred verticals, and current pipeline gaps, and focusing outbound effort accordingly
Prepare and deliver clear, structured meeting briefs for AEs prior to each booked discovery call — summarising the prospect's role, company context, key business challenges, and the conversation that led to the meeting
Participate in joint account planning sessions with AEs — contributing outbound intelligence, contact mapping, and engagement history to inform IBERIA territory strategy

Omilia Platform and Market Knowledge

Develop and maintain a working knowledge of Omilia's product portfolio — OCP, miniApps, Pathfinder, CoPilot, TalkGuard, and OptimizeIQ — sufficient to hold an intelligent first conversation with a senior prospect, articulate Omilia's key differentiators, and connect platform capabilities to common contact centre pain points across IBERIA verticals
Maintain awareness of GDPR and the EU AI Act as they relate to enterprise AI and contact centre deployments — sufficient to address basic compliance questions in initial prospect conversations and position Omilia credibly in the European regulatory context
Stay current with Omilia's IBERIA target verticals, competitive landscape, and key industry trends — ensuring that outreach messaging reflects current market context and positions Omilia credibly against incumbent CCaaS and IVR vendors active in European markets

Reporting and Process

Maintain Salesforce as the system of record for all prospecting activity — logging calls, emails, LinkedIn touches, meetings booked, and pipeline progression accurately and consistently
Track and report on personal KPIs — including outreach volume, connection rates, meeting booking rates, and pipeline contribution — and use this data to continuously refine messaging, sequencing, and targeting across IBERIA markets
Participate in regular pipeline reviews and coaching sessions with the Sales Director — sharing what is working, identifying regional blockers, and incorporating feedback to improve performance
Occasionally travel within IBERIA to support trade shows, industry events, and internal sales team meetings — up to 10% of the time

Requirements

Required:

1-3 years of experience in a sales development, business development, or outbound prospecting role — preferably within enterprise SaaS, CCaaS, conversational AI, or B2B technology
Demonstrated track record of meeting or exceeding outbound prospecting and meeting-booking targets in a structured SDR or BDR environment
Proficiency with outbound sales tools — including Salesforce CRM, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, and sales engagement platforms (e.g., Outreach, Salesloft, Apollo)
Strong written and verbal communication skills in English, Spanish and Portuguese — able to craft personalised, compelling cold outreach and hold confident discovery conversations with senior CX, IT, and operations decision-makers across IBERIA markets
Awareness of and sensitivity to the distinct business cultures, procurement norms, and communication styles across key IBERIA markets — and the ability to adapt outreach tone and approach accordingly
Organised and self-directed work style — able to manage a high volume of outbound activity across multiple accounts and geographies without losing attention to quality or personalisation
Genuine curiosity about enterprise technology, contact centre automation, and conversational AI — and the motivation to develop deep product and market knowledge over time
Coachable and growth-oriented — open to feedback, willing to iterate on messaging and approach, and committed to continuous improvement
Bachelor's degree in Business, Communications, Marketing, or a related discipline

Nice to have:

Fluency in one or more European languages (German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch) — directly relevant for outreach into non-English-speaking IBERIA markets
Prior exposure to contact centre technology, CCaaS, IVR, or conversational AI — either through sales experience or personal interest
Familiarity with Omilia's platform or competing platforms (Genesys, Avaya, Nuance/Microsoft, Google CCAI, Amazon Connect)
Experience prospecting into large enterprise accounts across IBERIA financial services, insurance, telecommunications, or utilities verticals
