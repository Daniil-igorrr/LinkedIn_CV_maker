About the job
My Local Courier, Delivery Solutions Network is looking for an early-career B2B Sales Representative interested in helping grow a new B2B operational solution.
We want this to be an opportunity where the salesperson can grow with the business. As sales and the business grow, our intention is to increase compensation and create greater earning opportunities for the person helping us build that growth.
We are looking for someone with genuine sales experience who is coachable, persistent, comfortable speaking with business owners and operations leaders, and motivated to close business.
What you'll do• Contact pre-qualified prospects provided to you• Start conversations with business owners and operations decision-makers• Follow up consistently and professionally• Present the solution clearly and close new customers• Provide feedback on what prospect information and sales intelligence would help you have stronger conversations and sell more effectively• Communicate what you are learning from prospect conversations so we can continually improve the information and support provided to you
What we're looking for• Some experience in B2B sales, outbound sales, appointment setting, business development, inside sales, or a related sales role• Comfortable communicating by phone and email• Persistent follow-up without being pushy• Professional communication with business decision-makers• Able to work independently and communicate clearly about what you need to succeed• Someone still building their sales career who wants an opportunity to demonstrate what they can do
CompensationStarting at 15% commission on each sale. This is an independent, commission-only opportunity. Qualified prospects are provided. As sales and the business grow, there is opportunity for increased compensation and earning potential.
