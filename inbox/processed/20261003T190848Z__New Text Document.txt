About the job
Company Description Knurek Investigations (PI Agency #: A2500448) is a licensed and insured investigative agency providing professional services throughout Florida. The agency focuses on criminal defense investigations, theft and fraud inquiries, and comprehensive due diligence and background checks. It also specializes in hard-to-locate skip trace investigations, serving law firms, businesses, and private clients. Knurek Investigations is committed to thorough, discreet, and ethical investigative work, offering a free consultation to help clients understand their options. Team members contribute to delivering accurate information that supports informed decisions and legal outcomes.Role Description This is an independent contractor remote salesperson role focused on generating new business and nurturing relationships for Knurek Investigations. Day-to-day responsibilities include identifying and qualifying leads, reaching out to prospective clients via phone, email, and virtual meetings, and clearly explaining the agency’s investigative services. The Salesperson will prepare proposals and quotes, follow up on inquiries, and maintain an organized pipeline using CRM or tracking tools. The role also involves collaborating with investigators to understand service capabilities, updating management on sales performance, and ensuring a professional, compliant approach to all client interactions.Qualifications
Strong skills in prospecting, lead generation, and consultative selling tailored to professional services.
Ability to communicate clearly and persuasively in writing and in virtual or phone-based conversations.
Experience managing a sales pipeline using CRM or similar tools and maintaining accurate records.
Knowledge of or interest in legal, investigative, or professional services industries is beneficial.
Demonstrated ability to work independently in a remote environment, meet targets, and manage time effectively.
High ethical standards, discretion, and professionalism when handling sensitive client information.
Preferred: prior experience in B2B or B2C sales, especially in investigative, legal, or security-related services.
Preferred: associate or bachelor’s degree in Business, Marketing, Criminal Justice, or a related field, or equivalent experience.
