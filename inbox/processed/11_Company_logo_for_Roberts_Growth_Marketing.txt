About the job
Campus Growth Marketing RepresentativeLocation: Remote / FlexiblePay: Commission OnlySchedule: Flexible
I’m hiring a Growth Marketing Representative to help with user acquisition, lead generation, and affiliate marketing for sportsbook referral offers.I work with referral offers across platforms such as DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Hard Rock, and other sportsbook/gaming apps. Your job is to find interested users, explain how the signup process works, answer their questions, and get qualified leads ready to complete a referral through me.This is a performance-based role that combines growth marketing, sales, business development, and customer acquisition.

What You’ll Do
Generate leads through your own network, friend groups, teams, workplaces, organizations, and other outreach methods
Identify and reach out to potential new users
Explain sportsbook signup offers and the referral process
Confirm leads meet the age and location requirements for the specific platform
Qualify interested leads and get them ready to complete their signup
Send qualified leads to me so I can handle the referral link, verification, and submission process
Follow up with interested leads who have not completed the process
Find new ways to reach larger groups and generate more signups
You will not be given a list of people to contact. Lead generation and outreach are a major part of the role. I can provide outreach ideas, scripts, strategies, and guidance, but you will have the freedom to develop your own approach.

CompensationThis is a 100% commission-based role with uncapped earnings.
$10 per approved signup
+$100 bonus for every 30 approved signups
No earnings cap
A signup is considered approved once the user completes the required referral process and the referral is accepted.

Who I’m Looking ForPrevious marketing or sales experience is not required.This could be a strong fit for someone interested in gaining hands-on experience in growth marketing, sales, affiliate marketing, user acquisition, lead generation, or business development.The ideal person is:
Highly social with a strong personal network
Involved in Greek life, fraternities or sororities, student organizations, clubs, sports teams, workplaces, or other large groups
Comfortable with networking and reaching out to friends, acquaintances, and new people
Self-motivated and comfortable working independently
Competitive and motivated by performance-based compensation

Leadership experience is a huge plus. Fraternity and sorority members, club officers, student organization leaders, team captains, campus ambassadors, and other highly connected people could be especially strong fits for the role.
