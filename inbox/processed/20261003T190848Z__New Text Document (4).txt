About the job
Company Description Benchmark Ledger Solutions provides virtual bookkeeping and financial clarity for small businesses and nonprofits across West Michigan, focusing on organizations within their first five years of operation. The firm helps owners understand their numbers, stay organized, and make confident decisions through accurate reporting and practical education. Core services include transaction recording, reconciliation, accounts payable and receivable management, financial reporting, budgeting, and performance benchmarking. Additional offerings such as catch-up bookkeeping, business dashboards, notary services, and client training workshops are designed to simplify financial management. Guided by a clear mission, Benchmark Ledger Solutions turns financial data into direction, giving business owners clarity, control, and confidence in every financial decision.
Role Description This is a full-time remote role for a B2B Sales Consultant. The B2B Sales Consultant will identify and qualify prospective small business and nonprofit clients, conduct discovery calls, and present tailored virtual bookkeeping and financial clarity solutions. Responsibilities include managing the sales pipeline, preparing proposals, coordinating demos, and following up with leads to drive new business and long-term relationships. The role involves collaborating closely with the bookkeeping and operations teams to ensure a smooth onboarding experience and high client satisfaction. The B2B Sales Consultant will also gather feedback from clients, contribute to refining service offerings, and support outreach campaigns and partner relationships across West Michigan.
Qualifications
Candidates should possess strong Sales Consulting and Consulting skills, with experience in B2B solution-based selling.
Candidates should possess Customer Service and Customer Satisfaction skills, with a focus on relationship-building and retention.
Candidates should possess excellent Communication skills, including clear written and verbal communication and active listening.
Experience selling professional services, financial or accounting solutions, or SaaS offerings is beneficial.
Ability to work independently in a remote environment, manage time effectively, and prioritize multiple opportunities.
Comfort with virtual meeting tools, CRM systems, and basic financial and bookkeeping concepts is preferred.
Prior experience in small business, nonprofit, or West Michigan regional markets is an advantage.
Relevant education in business, marketing, communications, or a related field is helpful but not required with equivalent experience.

Compensation This role is commission-based; on average, you will receive anywhere between 30% and 50% of the monthly service package price. Our sales consultants usually get paid out $3,000 for making 7 sales, but actual compensation depends on the sales volume. Payroll happens every 2 weeks through direct deposit. No additional benefits are offered, and taxes will not be withheld from the compensation paid out.
