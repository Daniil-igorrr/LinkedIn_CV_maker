About the job
Blasen Capital is expanding its national business development team and is seeking experienced sales professionals to represent our proprietary capital formation program.We work with companies, investment managers, real estate sponsors and other issuers accessing the private capital markets. Our program brings together the professional resources and services required to prepare, launch and support a capital raise.
The RoleThis is a sales and relationship-development position, not an investment banking execution role. You will develop prospective clients, build referral and industry relationships, present our capital formation program and close new engagements.
Blasen Capital provides the program, training, sales materials and professional team supporting client engagements.
Who We're Looking ForYou do not need to be an investment banker or hold a securities license, but you must already understand the language and environment of finance and private capital.Relevant experience may include financial services, private markets, alternative investments, fund services, fintech, capital markets, investor relations, commercial finance or related B2B sales and business development.We are looking for self-directed professionals who can develop their own pipeline, communicate credibly with investment managers and business executives, and actively build relationships through direct outreach, professional networks, conferences and industry events.
Compensation & OpportunityThis is a 100% commission position with uncapped compensation.
Successful representatives will have the opportunity to take on larger roles within Blasen Capital. 
Top producers may pursue securities licensing and, when appropriately licensed, become eligible to participate in compensation from qualifying capital transactions generated through their client relationships.
Position DetailsLocation Remote, anywhere in the United States
Travel Industry conferences, networking and business development as appropriate
