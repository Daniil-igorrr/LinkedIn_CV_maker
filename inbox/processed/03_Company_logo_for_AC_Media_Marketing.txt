About the job
Company Description AC Media & Marketing is a boutique marketing firm that provides strategic consulting, fractional marketing leadership, and campaign and media management services. The company partners with businesses, organizations, and teams to strengthen their marketing capabilities through clear strategy and creative problem-solving. AC Media & Marketing focuses on practical, hands-on execution to turn ideas into measurable results. Team members collaborate closely with clients to align marketing efforts with business goals and drive growth. The firm values independence, accountability, and a client-centered approach in all engagements.Role Description The Sales Growth Lead is a remote contract role responsible for driving new business opportunities and expanding existing client accounts. Day-to-day activities include prospecting and qualifying leads, conducting discovery calls, and presenting AC Media & Marketing’s services to potential clients. This role manages the sales pipeline, prepares proposals, and coordinates with internal teams to ensure smooth onboarding and handoff to delivery. The Sales Growth Lead tracks performance metrics, refines outreach strategies, and provides regular reporting on sales activity and revenue projections. The role also involves maintaining strong relationships with decision-makers, identifying upsell and cross-sell opportunities, and contributing feedback to improve service offerings and go-to-market efforts.Qualifications 
Sales and business development skills, including lead generation, pipeline management, and closing new business.
Experience with consultative selling, client discovery, and presenting marketing or professional services solutions.
Knowledge of marketing and media concepts, such as campaign strategy, branding, and digital marketing fundamentals.
Strong communication and relationship-building abilities, including negotiation, stakeholder management, and client retention.
Comfort working in a remote, contract capacity with self-directed time management and consistent follow-through.
Proficiency with CRM tools, sales enablement platforms, and basic productivity software (e.g., spreadsheets, presentation tools).
Prior experience in a marketing agency, consulting firm, or professional services environment is beneficial.
Bachelor’s degree in Business, Marketing, Communications, or equivalent experience in sales and client development.
