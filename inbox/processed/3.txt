About the job
We are an international online education platform offering personalized lessons with tutors from around the world.
We are currently looking for a highly organized and execution-driven Customer Success & Retention Specialist to join our team remotely.
About the Role
This is not a traditional customer support role. The position combines:
Customer Support
Customer Success
Retention & Reactivation
Subscription & Billing Operations

Your main responsibility will be to manage student communication, resolve issues quickly, and actively reduce cancellations and churn.
Key Responsibilities
Handle daily communication with students via WhatsApp, phone, and email
Manage subscription-related requests (upgrades, downgrades, changes)
Handle cancellation requests and apply retention strategies
Follow up with inactive students and re-engage them
Manage student backlog and reduce churn risk
Ensure all tickets are followed up and resolved (no pending cases)
Coordinate with tutors and internal teams when needed

What Success Looks Like
Fast response times
No overdue tickets
High follow-up consistency
Reduction in cancellations
Increased reactivation of inactive students

Ideal Candidate
Experience in customer support, customer success, and/or retention roles
Strong organizational and multitasking skills
Experience with subscription-based services or billing is a strong plus
Comfortable working with CRM systems (e.g. HubSpot etc.)
Proactive mindset – you follow up until the issue is resolved
Ability to work independently and take ownership

This Role Is NOT for You If
You wait for customers instead of following up
You struggle with organization or task management
You prefer slow-paced or purely communication-based roles

Compensation€1,100–€1,300/month (Freelance Contractor) + performance bonus
What We’re Looking For
We’re looking for someone who wants to build a long-term career with us — not someone who is simply looking for their next job.
You’ll thrive in this role if you:
Take ownership without waiting to be told what to do.
Care about solving problems, not just completing tasks.
Follow through until every issue is fully resolved.
Enjoy improving processes and making things better.
Want to have a real impact on the growth of the company.

This is a role for someone who is reliable, proactive, and genuinely committed to delivering an outstanding experience for our students.
Hiring Process
The hiring process includes:
A practical assessment (test) based on real scenarios
Interview for selected candidates

Additional Information
Fully remote position
Freelance collaboration (independent contractor / invoice-based cooperation)
