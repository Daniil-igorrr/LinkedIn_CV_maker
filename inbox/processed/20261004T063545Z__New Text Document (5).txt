About the job
The RoleWork with warm leads from individuals and families who have requested information about insurance coverage. You will speak with prospective clients, understand their needs, review available options, prepare quotes, and guide them through the application process.
This role combines consultative sales with ongoing account management. You will manage multiple prospects throughout the day, follow up consistently, and keep each application moving toward completion. Training, sales resources, and team support are provided from day one.
What the Work Looks Like• Contact warm leads and learn what type of coverage they are looking for.• Review insurance options and explain plan differences, costs, and benefits.• Prepare quotes and make coverage recommendations based on client needs.• Follow up with prospects and keep opportunities moving forward.• Complete applications and verify information for accuracy.• Maintain clear notes and track each account through the sales process.
What We're Looking For• Strong communication skills and confidence speaking with prospective clients.• Organized and able to manage multiple leads and follow-ups throughout the day.• Comfortable discussing insurance options and making recommendations.• Detail-oriented when preparing quotes and completing applications.• Motivated by goals and performance-based compensation.• Dependable internet and a quiet work-from-home setup.• Comfortable working independently in a structured full-time remote role.
What's Included• Warm leads from individuals actively seeking information.• Training and product education provided from day one.• Sales resources, quoting tools, and established processes.• Ongoing coaching and support from a team lead.• Flexible work-from-home environment.• Performance-based compensation with commission opportunities.
